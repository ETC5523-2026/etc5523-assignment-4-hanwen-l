# bushfireheat: Explore Fire-Season Heat in South-Eastern Australia

Daily maximum temperature and rainfall from the ERA5 reanalysis for
eight towns in south-eastern Australia affected by the 2019/20 Black
Summer fires. Functions count hot days in each September to February
fire season, compare a recent period with a mid-century baseline and
plot the result, and a Shiny app lets users explore how often extreme
fire-season heat now occurs and how sensitive that change is to the
temperature threshold.
